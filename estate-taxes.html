Washington probate · Managing an estate

What taxes must be handled during Washington probate?

Prepared by Ruth Apahidean West, Queen Anne Law, PLLC

Quick answer

Estate administration may involve the decedent's final individual income tax return, an estate or trust income tax return, and Washington or federal estate tax returns. The applicable filings depend on the estate and the date of death.

Final individual return

Review the decedent's filing history, income and tax documents and coordinate any required final Form 1040.

Income after death

Income or gains received by an estate may require Form 1041. Keep date-of-death valuations and estate account records; distributions can have tax consequences for beneficiaries.

Estate tax versus inheritance

Estate tax is distinct from a tax on inheritance and from income tax on inherited retirement funds. Washington and federal estate tax thresholds and rules should be checked for the year of death.

Work with a tax professional early

Complicated retirement accounts, real estate sales and taxable estates can benefit from coordinated legal and tax advice. Do not close the estate before addressing tax liabilities.

Common questions

Is inherited money always tax-free?

No. The nature of the asset, income and applicable estate tax rules matter.

What estate tax exemption applies?

The exemption depends on the applicable law and the date of death. Confirm the Washington and federal thresholds separately.

Washington legal sources

  • RCW 11.48.010
