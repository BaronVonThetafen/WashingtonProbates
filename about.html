Our approach

Our guides explain Washington probate in plain language and link to relevant provisions of the Revised Code of Washington. Statutes and court procedures can change, and each estate has its own facts. Washington Probates is operated by Queen Anne Law, PLLC and is not affiliated with any government agency.

Using this resource

Use the guides to identify questions, understand general requirements and prepare for a consultation. The information is educational and does not replace advice from an attorney about your particular estate.

About the practice

Ruth Apahidean West holds undergraduate degrees in English and History and a Juris Doctor from the University of Washington. Queen Anne Law, PLLC provides legal services for probate, trusts, wills, estate planning and related administration. Ruth is based in Seattle and also spends time in Pierce County. She welcomes probate inquiries across Washington and can associate with other attorneys when local counsel or an appearance in another county is needed. Depending on the estate and the scope of work, the firm may offer hourly, flat-fee or hybrid billing. Ruth’s usual hourly rate for probate work is $475. After an initial screening, the fact-finding meeting is billed only if you decide to move forward with her. For estate planning, her usual hourly rate is $450, and she offers a discounted initial consultation. Estate planning consultation terms are separate from the probate fact-finding meeting policy. Contact the firm to discuss service availability and potential fee arrangements.
