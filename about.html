About Washington Probate Guide

An educational resource operated by Queen Anne Law, a Seattle estate planning and probate practice.

Our approach

The guides explain Washington probate in plain language, with links to relevant statutes, court resources and educational research. King County Probates served as a research starting point; its content is not presented as our own original legal authority. This site is not affiliated with the courts or the original guide.

Editorial process

The draft content has been independently written and is awaiting attorney review. Legal accuracy, local procedure, attribution and publication permissions must be confirmed before release.
