When a consultation is particularly helpful

Consider legal advice when title to a home is unclear, there is no will, a bank requires letters, the representative lives out of state, beneficiaries disagree or the estate may be insolvent.

What to ask an attorney

Ask about the necessary filings, court authority, notice deadlines, costs, and how legal work will be divided between counsel and the representative. Confirm which counties and types of matters the firm accepts.

About Queen Anne Law

The firm's supplied homepage identifies Ruth as a University of Washington graduate in English and History and a UW law graduate. The practice also assists with wills, trusts and powers of attorney.

Prepare for the first appointment

Bring available estate documents, a list of assets and debts, names of potential heirs, and your main questions. Do not send confidential details through an unsecured channel.
