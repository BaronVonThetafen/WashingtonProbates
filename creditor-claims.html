How do creditor claims work in Washington probate?

Prepared for Queen Anne Law · Attorney review pending

Quick answer

A representative must address estate debts. Publishing a notice to creditors may shorten claim periods, but publication alone is not a substitute for a reasonable search for identifiable creditors and the required direct notices.

Publication

The guide describes publication in a qualifying legal newspaper once weekly for three consecutive weeks. Verify the proper county and publication before filing.

Search for creditors

Review statements, loan records, correspondence and other financial documents. Document your search and provide actual notice when required.

Claim periods

The source describes a four-month period after first publication and a 30-day actual-notice period, with the later deadline applicable in specified circumstances. Other rules apply when a creditor is not notified; do not assume all debts expire on one date.

Allowing and rejecting claims

Evaluate claims for validity, deadlines and priority. A rejected claim can trigger a separate litigation deadline. Get legal advice before paying disputed claims or distributing assets.

Common questions

Do heirs personally owe a deceased relative's debts?

Not simply because they are heirs. Joint obligations, guarantees and property rules can create different issues.

Is it safe to pay beneficiaries before claims are resolved?

Doing so can expose the representative to risk. Determine obligations and appropriate reserves first.
