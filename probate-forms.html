What documents do I need to open Washington probate?

Prepared for Queen Anne Law · Attorney review pending

Quick answer

The necessary papers depend on whether there is a will, who will serve, whether advance notice is required and what authority the representative requests. An opening packet commonly includes a petition, proposed order, oath and relevant supporting documents.

In this guide

For an estate with a will

Collect the original will and any codicils, petition for appointment, proposed order admitting the will, oath and supporting proof of death as required locally.

For an estate without a will

Prepare an intestate petition addressing heir identification, appointment priority and asset investigation. Check the updated 2026 requirements and bond rules.

Situation-specific documents

A resident-agent designation, hearing notice, waivers, witness declaration or guardian ad litem order may be necessary.

Later documents

Notices, declarations of mailing, creditor papers, inventory, receipts and closing documents each serve different purposes. Use current forms suited to the facts; this guide does not provide blank pleadings.

Common questions

Can I just use an old petition I found online?

Not safely without checking current law, local practice and estate-specific facts.

Do I file the original will?

The original is normally important to admission; confirm filing and retention requirements with the court.
