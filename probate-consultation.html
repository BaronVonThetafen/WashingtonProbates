How your initial probate meeting works

Ruth first determines whether your matter appears to be a good fit. If so, she arranges a fact-finding meeting to discuss the estate and the legal work involved.

The initial fact-finding meeting is billed only if you decide to move forward with the firm. If you choose not to proceed, you are not billed for that meeting. No advance consultation payment is requested by this website.

Probate representation may be billed hourly, for a flat fee, or under a hybrid arrangement, depending on the estate and the agreed scope of work. Fees are discussed before engagement.
