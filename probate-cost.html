How much does a Washington probate attorney cost?

Prepared for Queen Anne Law · Attorney review pending

Quick answer

Probate legal fees depend on the scope of work and the estate's complexity. Attorneys may bill hourly, quote a flat fee for defined work or provide limited-scope assistance. Queen Anne Law's existing website lists a $225 one-hour initial consultation and a $450 standard hourly rate; confirm current fees when booking.

Hourly arrangements

You pay for time worked. Ask how the attorney estimates total fees, what tasks other staff may perform and when invoices are issued.

Fixed or limited scope

A flat fee sets a price for defined services; limited-scope work might involve drafting or reviewing a petition while the client performs other duties. Ask which services are included and how changes in scope are handled. These are general fee models, not promises that Queen Anne Law offers each.

Other estate expenses

Court filing, letters, publication, bond, appraisals and tax preparation may be separate expenses. Actual amounts depend on the case and current provider charges.

Who pays?

Reasonable legal expenses incurred in estate administration may be paid or reimbursed by the estate, subject to applicable law. A representative must use estate funds responsibly; confirm payment terms in writing.

Common questions

Is $225 the total cost of probate?

No. It is the consultation price shown in the supplied Queen Anne Law homepage, not the full administration fee.

Can I get a quote before hiring?

Use a consultation to discuss the estate's assets, heirs, creditors, documents, any litigation and your desired scope of representation.
